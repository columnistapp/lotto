Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and why.

1. Information processed

1.1 Account and sign-in

Account and authentication information is processed when you create an account or sign in.

1.2 Referrals, rewards, and purchases

When referral and in-app reward features are used, referral and reward records are processed. Purchase and entitlement information is also processed as needed to provide access to purchased packages and features.

2. Analysis data

2.1 On-device processing

Numerical data you enter or import, analysis settings, and results are processed on your device.

2.2 Transfer to servers

This content is not sent to the app's servers for analysis.

3. Purposes of processing

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • Manage accounts and sign-in.
  • Apply referrals and in-app rewards.
  • Provide access to purchased packages and features.
  • Display advertising and protect service security.

4. Advertising services

4.1 Purpose

In versions of the Application that display ads, advertising SDKs are used to serve and measure ads and help prevent abuse.

4.2 Data that may be processed

Depending on the provider and device settings, advertising SDKs may process:

  • IP address and network information.
  • Device and app information, including device or advertising identifiers.
  • Ad impressions and clicks.
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

5. Purchases

5.1 Purchase transactions

In-app packages and feature purchases are made through the relevant official app stores.

5.2 Payment information

The Application does not collect or store payment-card or bank-account details. Payment transactions and records associated with app-store accounts are governed by the privacy statements and retention practices of the relevant app stores.

6. Data retention

Account, referral, reward, and purchase records are retained for as long as needed to provide the service, manage account and feature access, maintain security, and complete necessary operations.

7. Regions where data is processed

The primary data region for account and authentication infrastructure is Frankfurt, Germany. Sign-in, advertising, and app-store providers may also process data in other countries depending on their services and configurations.
