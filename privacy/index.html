Privacy Policy

Version: 1.1 · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use Columnist: Lotto Analytics AI and its related online services. Some features operate on your device, while features such as accounts, purchases, notifications and advertising may rely on relevant online services. The data processed may vary depending on the features and platform you use.

1. Data controller

1.1. The data controller responsible for processing personal data in connection with Columnist: Lotto Analytics AI is Halil Acar, the developer of the Application. Contact: columnist.app@gmail.com

2. What data is processed?

2.1. Data may be provided directly by you, generated while the Application operates, or obtained from third-party services you select. The main categories of data are:

2.2. Account and session data. Account identifier, email address, identifiers and profile information provided by the sign-in provider you select, verification status, and tokens required for session security.

2.3. Referrals, purchases and access. Referral codes and referral relationships, reward conditions, store and product information, purchase-verification records, and access status for paid features.

2.4. Device, notifications and preferences. Notification token, platform and Application information, notification language, and Application settings such as language, theme and notification preferences.

2.5. Advertising. In versions where advertising is enabled, advertising providers (listed in Section 4) may process IP address, device and Application information, advertising/device identifiers, and information about ad requests, impressions and interactions. Approximate regional information may be inferred from the IP address.

2.6. Technical records and diagnostic logs. The Application may create technical event and diagnostic records on your device to identify errors and operational issues. These records may include the Application version, platform, event time, error type and limited technical status information. Their scope is limited; passwords, session or purchase tokens, and personal analysis/lottery content are not included in routine diagnostic logs. Logs are stored on your device. The diagnostic screen becomes available only when the developer grants the required access to your account. Logs are not automatically sent to the developer or retrieved remotely from your device; viewing, exporting and sharing them are controlled by the user.

2.7. Local analysis and external sources. Game/lottery data, imported files, analysis and simulation parameters, work history and results are processed and stored on your device. When you connect to external data sources that you select within the Application, those sources may receive the technical information necessary for the connection.

2.8. Support and requests. When you email us, your email address, message and any attached files are processed to respond to your request.

5. Retention, account deletion and data security

5.1. Data is retained for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, taking into account applicable legal retention obligations. When the purpose no longer applies, data is deleted, destroyed or genuinely anonymized so that it can no longer be linked to an individual.

5.2. Account deletion

5.2.1. Account deletion is initiated from within the Application and may require re-authentication for security. During deletion, profile, session, referral, reward, notification-device and access-right records associated with the account are removed. Deleting the account does not delete analysis, lottery or work files on your device; those contents remain stored on your device.

5.3. A limited purchase-verification summary with its account link removed may be retained to protect purchase security or prevent the same purchase entitlement from being used with another account. The fact that this summary is stored as a hash does not, by itself, mean that it is anonymous; it is retained only for as long as the purpose of preventing reuse of the same purchase entitlement continues.

5.4. A limited record documenting completion of the deletion process, which does not contain the user’s identity, may also be retained.

5.5. You can initiate an account-deletion request from within the Application. Deletion and other privacy requests submitted by email are handled within the periods prescribed by applicable law after the request has been securely verified. Deleting the account may not automatically cancel subscriptions managed by an app store; such subscriptions must be managed separately through the relevant store.

5.6. Security

Technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data and the risks are applied; access is restricted and data is processed only for the stated purposes. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure. Any necessary notifications and actions in the event of a security incident are handled in accordance with applicable law.
